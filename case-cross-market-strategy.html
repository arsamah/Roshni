Case study · Vehicle leasing

Driving cross-Scandinavian web and communication strategy

StrategyMobility

The situation

A Scandinavian vehicle leasing and mobility business operated separately across Norway, Sweden and Denmark — each market with its own web presence, its own tone, and its own way of doing things. Nothing was shared, which meant every market solved the same problems independently, and a new launch meant starting again from zero.

What we did

We built a single web and communications strategy the business could run across all three markets: shared structure, a consistent voice, and a common technical approach, with clear room left for local language, regulation and market maturity. That meant aligning stakeholders across every country team and sequencing the rollout by market readiness rather than forcing one launch date on everyone.

The result

One strategy instead of three, far less duplicated effort across markets, and a foundation built to make the next market — wherever it is — faster to launch than the last.
