Case study · Consumer finance

Leading digital platform modernisation across the Nordic leasing business

PlatformFinancial services

The situation

A leading Nordic financial services group's leasing business was running its digital operations on a platform that had grown, patch by patch, over more than a decade. Every new product, integration, or regulatory change took longer than it should have — and the gap between what customers expected and what the platform could deliver was widening.

What we did

Our founding team led the modernisation programme end to end: auditing the existing platform and its dependencies, mapping a phased migration path that kept the business running throughout, and redesigning the core workflows around how customers and internal teams actually used the product. Because this was a regulated financial services platform, compliance and risk teams were involved from day one rather than brought in at the end.

The result

A modernised platform the business can build on rather than work around — faster to ship changes to, simpler to maintain, and structured to support new products as the leasing business grows.
